Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

No accounts, no personal data

PlayHub does not require an account and does not collect names, emails or any personal information from you to play.

Cookies

PlayHub itself only uses cookies where they are necessary to make the site work (for example, remembering your preferences). Third-party services on this site (described below) may set cookies on your device to deliver and measure advertisements, or to analyse traffic.

Embedded games (Playpager)

Easy Chess, Checkers and Ludo are embedded from Playpager and run inside an iframe on those pages. Playpager may set its own cookies or collect its own usage data when you play those games — that happens under Playpager's own privacy policy, not ours. The rest of our games run entirely in your browser with no embedded third party.

Third-party advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this website. Google and its partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure how effective the ads are.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies is explained on Google's Advertising page.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use simple, aggregated analytics to understand which games people enjoy. Aggregated data is used only to improve the site and is not used to identify you.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@playhubgames.com, or visit our Contact page.
