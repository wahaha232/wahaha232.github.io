Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

No accounts, no personal data

PlayHub does not require an account and does not collect names, emails or any personal information from you to play.

Cookies

PlayHub itself only uses cookies and local storage where they are necessary to make the site work (for example, remembering whether you accepted the cookie notice). Third-party advertising services (described below) may set cookies or similar identifiers on your device to deliver and measure advertisements.

Third-party advertising

This website displays advertising delivered by third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and they are loaded only after you accept the cookie notice on the site.

  • You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info.

Analytics

This site does not use third-party analytics services and does not profile its visitors.
