How to play Pyramid Solitaire

  • 28 cards form a pyramid of 7 rows. A card is only available once the cards resting on it have been removed.
  • Click two available cards whose values add up to 13 to remove both of them (Ace = 1, Jack = 11, Queen = 12).
  • A King counts as 13 and is discarded by itself.
  • The top card of the waste pile can be paired with an available pyramid card.
  • Click the stock pile to turn over the next card. Clear the entire pyramid to win.
