How to play Spider Solitaire

  • 104 cards (two decks) are dealt into 10 columns; only the top card of each column starts face up.
  • You may place a card on a card of one higher rank, regardless of suit.
  • A run of the same suit in descending order can be moved together.
  • Assemble a complete same-suit sequence King → Ace and it is removed automatically. Clear eight sequences to win.
  • Click the deck to deal a new row of 10 face-up cards (one per column).
