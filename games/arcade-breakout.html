🧱 Breakout

Play Free Online · Arcade

Score: 0 Lives: 3

Move the paddle to keep the ball in play and destroy every brick. You have 3 lives; you lose one whenever the ball drops past your paddle.

How to play Breakout

  • Move the paddle left and right with the arrow keys or A/D (or the on-screen buttons).
  • Press Space (or Launch) to send the ball up from the paddle.
  • Each brick you hit is worth 10 points. Hitting the ball with the edge of the paddle changes its angle.
  • You have 3 lives. Clear every brick to win.
