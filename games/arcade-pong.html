🏓 Pong

Play Free Online · Arcade

You: 0 CPU: 0

You are the blue paddle on the left. Keep the ball past the computer's paddle to score. The first player to 5 points wins.

How to play Pong

  • Move your paddle up and down with the arrow keys or W/S (or the on-screen buttons).
  • Return the ball; where it hits your paddle changes the angle it bounces off at.
  • Score a point when the ball passes the computer's paddle on the right.
  • First to 5 points wins the match.
