How to play Snakes & Ladders

  • Players take turns rolling a single die and moving their token forward that many squares, starting from square 1.
  • Land on the base of a ladder (blue outline) and you climb straight up to the square at its top.
  • Land on the head of a snake (red outline) and you slide down to the square at its tail.
  • Rolling a 6 earns an extra roll. If a roll would take you past square 100, it's wasted — you stay put and play passes to the other player.
  • The first player to land on square 100 exactly wins.
