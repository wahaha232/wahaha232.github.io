💣 Minesweeper

Play Free Online · 9 × 9, 10 Mines

Mines left: 10

Reveal squares to find the safe ones. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that square. Your first click is always safe.

How to play Minesweeper

  • Left-click (or tap) a square to reveal it. The first square you open is always mine-free.
  • A number shows how many of the eight surrounding squares contain a mine.
  • Right-click (or long-press on a touch screen) to place a flag on a square you think is a mine.
  • Reveal every square that is not a mine to win. Hitting a mine ends the game.
  • The counter shows how many mines are still unflagged.
