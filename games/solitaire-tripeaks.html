How to play TriPeaks Solitaire

  • 28 cards are laid out in three overlapping peaks; only cards with nothing resting on them are exposed.
  • Play an exposed card onto the waste if its rank is one higher or one lower than the top card — Aces and Kings wrap round (A ↔ K).
  • Click the stock pile to turn over a new waste card when you cannot play.
  • Clear all 28 layout cards to win.
