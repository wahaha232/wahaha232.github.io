How to play Dominoes

  • Each player starts with 7 tiles from a double-six set (28 tiles total); the rest form the draw pile (boneyard).
  • On your turn, play a tile from your hand onto either end of the line so that one of its numbers matches the exposed number at that end. Tap a tile, then choose which end if more than one is possible.
  • If you have no playable tile, draw from the boneyard until you can play — or until it runs out.
  • Only your own hand is shown; your opponent's tiles stay hidden until they're played, just like a real game.
  • First to play their last tile wins. If both players get stuck with an empty boneyard, whoever holds the fewest total pips wins.
