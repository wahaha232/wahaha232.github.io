How to play Backgammon

  • White moves counter-clockwise toward point 1 and bears off there; Black moves the opposite way toward point 24. Roll two dice each turn — doubles give you four moves of that number instead of two.
  • Tap one of your checkers, then tap a highlighted point to move it there using a matching die.
  • Landing on a point with a single opposing checker (a blot) sends it to the bar — that player must re-enter it before making any other move.
  • A point held by two or more opposing checkers is blocked and can't be landed on.
  • Once all 15 of your checkers are in your home board, you can start bearing them off. This version skips the doubling cube for simplicity.
  • First to bear off all 15 checkers wins.
