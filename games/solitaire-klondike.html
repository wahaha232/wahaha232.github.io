How to play Klondike Solitaire

  • Move cards in the tableau so they build down in alternating colors (red on black, black on red).
  • Only a King (or a sequence starting with a King) can be placed on an empty tableau column.
  • Build each foundation up by suit, from Ace to King.
  • Click the stock to draw cards into the waste; when the stock is empty, click it again to recycle the waste.
  • Double-click a card (or the waste top) to auto-move it to a foundation.
  • Move every card to the four foundations to win.
