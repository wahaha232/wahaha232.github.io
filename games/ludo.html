🎲 Ludo

Play Free Online · Two to Four Players

How to play Ludo

  • Each player has 4 tokens waiting in their yard. Roll a 6 to bring one out onto the board, landing on your own start square (a safe square).
  • On any other roll, move one of your tokens already on the board forward that many squares around the shared track.
  • Land exactly on an opponent's token on a non-safe square and you send it back to their yard.
  • Rolling a 6 earns an extra roll — but three 6s in a row forfeits your turn.
  • Once a token completes the lap, it turns into your own home stretch — you must roll the exact number to bring it all the way home.
  • First player to get all 4 tokens home wins.
