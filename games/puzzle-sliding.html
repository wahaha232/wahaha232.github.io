🔢 Sliding Puzzle

Play Free Online · 15-Puzzle

Moves: 0

Tap a tile next to the empty space to slide it, or use the arrow keys / WASD. Put the tiles back in order 1–15 with the empty space at the bottom-right.

How to play the Sliding Puzzle

  • The board holds the numbers 1–15 and one empty space.
  • Only tiles directly next to the empty space can slide into it.
  • Keep sliding until the numbers are in order (1–15 reading left to right, top to bottom) and the gap is at the bottom-right.
  • The puzzle is always solvable because it is shuffled with real moves from the finished state.
