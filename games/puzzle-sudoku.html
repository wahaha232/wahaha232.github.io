🧮 Sudoku

Play Free Online · 9 × 9 Logic Puzzle

Tap a square, then tap a number. Every row, column and 3×3 box must contain the digits 1–9 exactly once.

How to play Sudoku

  • Fill every empty square so each row, each column and each 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • Select a square and use the number pad (or your keyboard) to place a digit.
  • The dark grey squares are the given clues and cannot be changed.
  • A digit that breaks the rules is shown in red — correct it before you continue.
  • Pick a difficulty and press New Game for a fresh puzzle.
