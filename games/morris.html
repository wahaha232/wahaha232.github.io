How to play Nine Men's Morris

  • Placing phase: each player has 9 pieces. Take turns placing one on any empty point.
  • Line up 3 of your own pieces in a row along one of the board's lines (a "mill") and you immediately remove one of the opponent's pieces — pieces already in one of their mills are safe unless every one of their pieces is in a mill.
  • Moving phase: once all 18 pieces are placed, slide a piece to an empty point connected to it by a line. Forming a mill by moving also lets you remove a piece.
  • Flying: when you're down to exactly 3 pieces, you may move any piece to any empty point, not just an adjacent one.
  • You win by reducing the opponent to 2 pieces, or by leaving them with no legal move on their turn.
