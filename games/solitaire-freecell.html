How to play FreeCell

  • All 52 cards are dealt face up into 8 columns.
  • The four free cells (top-left) each hold a single card temporarily; the four foundations (top-right) are built up by suit from Ace to King.
  • In the tableau, cards build down in alternating colors — a red 6 goes on a black 7, and so on.
  • You can move a whole run at once if it is already in descending, alternating-color order. The length you can move depends on how many free cells and empty columns you have.
  • Move all 52 cards to the foundations to win.
