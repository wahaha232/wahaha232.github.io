👾 Space Invaders

Play Free Online · Arcade Shooter

← → move · Space to fire
Score: 0
Lives: 3

Move your ship with the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons, and press Space (or FIRE) to shoot. Clear every alien to win — but don't let them reach your row or shoot you down.

How to play Space Invaders

  • A fleet of aliens moves side to side above you and drops down one row each time it reaches the edge.
  • Move your ship left and right along the bottom, and fire straight up to destroy aliens.
  • The aliens get faster as fewer of them are left — clear them quickly.
  • Aliens fire back. You have 3 lives; each hit costs one life and resets your ship to the middle.
  • You win by destroying the whole fleet. You lose if your lives reach zero, or if the aliens reach your row.
