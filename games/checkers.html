How to play Checkers

  • Each side starts with 12 pieces on the dark squares of the three rows closest to them. Black moves first.
  • Regular pieces ("men") move one square diagonally forward only. To capture, jump diagonally over an adjacent opposing piece into the empty square beyond it — the jumped piece is removed.
  • Capturing is mandatory whenever it's available: if any of your pieces can jump, you must play a jump instead of a quiet move. If a piece can keep jumping after landing, it must keep going in the same turn.
  • Reach the farthest row and your piece is crowned a king, which can move and jump diagonally both forward and backward. Landing on the crowning row always ends your turn there, even if another jump would technically be possible.
  • You win when your opponent has no pieces left, or no legal move on their turn.
