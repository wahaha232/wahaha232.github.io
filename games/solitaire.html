About Solitaire

  • Klondike — the classic version: build tableau columns down in alternating colors and send Aces to the foundations.
  • FreeCell — every card is face up from the start; use the four free cells to sort everything out. Almost always solvable.
  • Spider — two decks across ten columns; collect same-suit runs from King down to Ace to clear them.
  • Pyramid — remove pairs of exposed cards that add up to 13. Kings go on their own.
  • TriPeaks — tap exposed cards one rank above or below the waste card; clear the three peaks.
