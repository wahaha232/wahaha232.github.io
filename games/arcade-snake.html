🐍 Snake

Play Free Online · Arcade

Score: 0

Steer the snake to eat the red food. Each bite makes you longer and scores a point. Don't hit the walls or your own tail.

How to play Snake

  • Use the arrow keys or WASD (or the on-screen pad on touch screens) to change direction.
  • Eat the food to grow longer and add a point to your score.
  • The snake keeps moving — you can't stop, and you can't immediately reverse into yourself.
  • The game ends if you run into a wall or your own body. Fill the whole board to win.
