How to play Battleship

  • Each player's 5 ships (lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, 2) are placed randomly at the start of the game.
  • Players alternate turns firing at a cell on the opponent's grid (right-hand board). A behind-the-scenes screen hides the boards between turns — pass the device and tap Ready when it's your turn.
  • Red means a hit, white means a miss. Once every cell of a ship has been hit, it's sunk.
  • The first player to sink the opponent's entire fleet wins.
