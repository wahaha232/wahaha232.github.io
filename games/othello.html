How to play Othello / Reversi

  • The game starts with 2 discs of each color in the center of the 8×8 board. Black always moves first.
  • You may only play on a square that "brackets" at least one straight line of the opponent's discs — meaning your new disc and an existing disc of yours trap one or more opposing discs between them, in a row, column or diagonal.
  • Every opposing disc trapped in a bracketed line flips to your color the moment you play.
  • If you have no legal move on your turn, it's skipped automatically and play passes to your opponent.
  • The game ends when neither player has a legal move anywhere on the board — usually when it's full. Whoever has the most discs of their color wins; equal counts is a draw.
