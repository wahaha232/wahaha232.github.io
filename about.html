Why we built it

The best games never get old. We believe classic games should be easy to find and even easier to play. PlayHub keeps things simple: one page full of games, one click, and you're playing.

Original games

Every game above is built from scratch by us: our own rules engine, our own board and controls, running entirely in your browser. We don't embed third-party game widgets.
