About Mindboard Games

Mindboard Games is a free online games portal. No account, no download, no sign-up — just pick a game and play instantly in your browser.

What we offer

Nine classic board and strategy games, all playable right now:

  • ♟ Chess
  • ⚫⚪ Othello / Reversi
  • ⛃ Checkers
  • 🎲 Ludo
  • 🎯 Backgammon
  • ⚫⚪ Nine Men's Morris
  • 🁫 Dominoes
  • 🚢 Classic Battleship
  • 🐍🪜 Snakes & Ladders

Why we built it

The best games never get old. We believe classic games should be easy to find and even easier to play. Mindboard Games keeps things simple: one page full of games, one click, and you're playing.

Original games

Every one of the nine games above is built from scratch by us: our own rules engine, our own board and controls, running entirely in your browser. We don't embed third-party game widgets.
