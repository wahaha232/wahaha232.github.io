Bringing Tokyo’s vibrant Shibuya Scramble Crossing directly to your screen, 24/7.

Welcome to Tokyo Live View

Our goal is to provide travelers, Tokyo enthusiasts, and virtual sightseers with a seamless 24/7 real-time view of the famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing, paired with up-to-date local weather information.

Whether you are planning your upcoming trip to Japan, checking local weather conditions, or simply enjoying the bustling urban scenery of Tokyo as background ambience, our live cam portal brings the heartbeat of Shibuya to you anytime, anywhere.

Thank you for visiting! Feel free to explore our travel links and enjoy the real-time live stream.
