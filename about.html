About This Project

Bringing Tokyo’s vibrant Shibuya Scramble Crossing directly to your screen, 24/7.

Welcome to Tokyo Live View

Our goal is to provide travelers, Tokyo enthusiasts, and virtual visitors with a seamless 24/7 real-time view of the famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing, together with updated local weather information.

Whether you are planning a trip to Japan, checking Tokyo conditions, or enjoying the atmosphere of one of the world's busiest intersections, our live camera brings the energy of Shibuya to your screen anytime.

Thank you for visiting Shibuya Live Cam. Explore Tokyo and enjoy the live stream experience.
