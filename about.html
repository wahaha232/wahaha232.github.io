About PlayHub

PlayHub is a free online games portal. No account, no download, no sign-up — just pick a game and play instantly in your browser.

What we offer

25 games are playable right now, in five categories:

  • Board Games — Easy Chess, Checkers, Ludo, Othello Reversi, Backgammon, Nine Men's Morris, Dominoes, Classic Battleship, Snakes & Ladders
  • Solitaire — Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, TriPeaks
  • Puzzle — 2048, Sliding Puzzle, Minesweeper, Sudoku
  • Arcade — Space Invaders, Snake, Breakout, Pong
  • Quiz — General Knowledge, Math, World Capitals

Why we built it

The best games never get old. We believe classic games should be easy to find and even easier to play. PlayHub keeps things simple: one page full of games, one click, and you're playing.

Original games

Every game on PlayHub is built from scratch by us: our own rules engine, our own board and controls, running entirely in your browser. We don't embed third-party game widgets.
